Skills and qualifications

Six areas an aspiring system administrator should develop, and why each matters.

Operating system administration

Servers run on Windows Server and Linux. Knowing how to install, configure and maintain them is the core of the job.

Networking

Services depend on networks. Understanding TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP and firewalls lets you find and fix connectivity problems.

Troubleshooting

Admins are called when things break. A structured approach finds causes faster and reduces downtime.

Security fundamentals

Access control, patching and least privilege protect data and users from threats.

Scripting and automation

Scripts save time and remove human error on repeated tasks such as account creation and backups.

Certifications

CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, Microsoft and Red Hat credentials prove knowledge to employers.
